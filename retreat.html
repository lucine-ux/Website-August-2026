What a retreat with me is like

Nothing to organise. Nobody needing anything from you.

Most of the folks who come hold everything together for everyone else. At a retreat, that stops for a while.

You lie down, close your eyes and breathe while I guide you. There are long gaps to rest. Someone else cooks. Nobody needs a thing from you.

You don't need any experience. Most people arrive a bit nervous, and that's normal.

Two ways it could look

I want to know which one you'd actually come to.

OPTION ONE

A Sydney day retreat

One day, close to home. Breathwork, rest and time to land, then home to your own bed. I ran a half day in Mosman in September, and this would be like that, with more time.

OPTION TWO

A full weekend away

Two nights away from everything, in the bush, 90 minutes from Sydney. Deep breathwork, movement, meals cooked for you and a small group. Like the October weekend.

Questions people ask

When is the next retreat?

Dates aren't set yet. I'm using the waitlist to decide whether to run a Sydney day retreat, a weekend away, or both. Waitlist members hear first.

Is it right for me if I've never done breathwork?

Yes. Most people who come have little or no breathwork experience. Every session is fully guided, and there's no right way to do it.

I'd be coming alone. Will I feel out of place?

Most guests come on their own and leave with connections they didn't expect. The groups are small, so it's easy to feel at home.

Is breathwork safe for me?

It isn't right for everyone. If you're pregnant, or you have a heart condition, epilepsy, high blood pressure, glaucoma or a psychiatric diagnosis, email me before you book so we can talk it through: lucine@backtobreath.net.au. This is a wellbeing practice. It isn't medical treatment, therapy or crisis support.
