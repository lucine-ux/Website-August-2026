You know what to do. Your body won't do it.

Your nervous system feels like it's always in a state of urgency. You're overthinking everything and your body is on high alert. It's been that way for so long that your body now thinks it's normal.

You KNOW what to do. You know you should stay calm. You know you should be the voice of reason. But your body can't make you do it.

That was me.

It was 8am. Lunch boxes half done. Breakfast on the table. Kids not dressed. I was trying to get everyone out the door and get myself ready too, and I could feel the clock ticking. I knew I should stay calm and be the voice of reason. But my heart and head were pounding, my chest and jaw were tight, and my voice got louder. I couldn't come back down. I would lose control, flip my lid, and feel so much guilt once the kids had left for the day. It felt extreme in my body, like a real emergency and a real threat.

For 37 years I thought I was the problem. I wasn't. My body was stuck on high alert, in survival mode.

What ADHDecompress is

2 weeks of live breathwork for ADHD nervous systems.

ADHDecompress is your first 2 weeks inside The Rewire. You start with 3 starter lessons. They're recorded, so you watch them when it suits you, before your first live session. Then you join my live sessions with me and settle in with a community that gets it.

So you can have:

  • Gentle breathwork in a safe room, so that your body can come down from fight or flight
  • A body that feels more ease and rest, so that you can feel more in control with the people you love
  • More room for things to go wrong, without losing control or flipping your lid

The breathing is gentle. Eyes closed. I help your body feel safe enough that stress can unwind at its own pace.

In each live session we set an intention together, then we breathe, then there's time to share what came up and ask questions.

Between sessions, you can set your intentions, share what you're noticing or ask a question in the community. If you can't join live, you watch the replay and do the same.

I spent two years training to teach this, because I couldn't keep it to myself.

I help ADHD folks regulate their nervous system, so they stop running on empty.

Questions

Can I start any time?

Yes. Your 2 weeks start the day you join.

Are the sessions really live?

Yes. They're my real twice weekly sessions, the same ones my Rewire members come to. Only the 3 starter lessons are recorded.

What if I can't make the live sessions?

Every session is recorded. You don't fall behind.

What if I've never done breathwork?

Most people haven't. It's gentle and I talk you through it.

What if I feel nothing?

That's okay. Sometimes it's big, sometimes very quiet.

What if big feelings come up?

I don't push for big feelings. You're in control and you can go as slowly as you like.

Is online okay?

Yes. You can do it from your bed.

Is this instead of therapy?

No. It sits alongside therapy and medication.

What happens after the 2 weeks?

You can stay on in The Rewire, my ongoing membership with two live sessions a week, a monthly workshop and a community. Or you can stop. Either is fine.

Is breathwork safe for me?

It isn't right for everyone. If you're pregnant, or you have a heart condition, epilepsy, high blood pressure, glaucoma or a psychiatric diagnosis, email me before you join so we can talk it through. This is a wellbeing practice. It isn't medical treatment, therapy or crisis support.

Still not sure?

Email me at lucine@backtobreath.net.au and tell me where you're at. I'll give you an honest answer.

Ready to finally come down?

You just need to lie down, close your eyes and let me talk you through it, so that your body can learn what safe feels like.

By the end of your 2 weeks, I hope your body feels a bit more rested. The noise inside a little quieter. Less urgency about everything. And you have something you can come back to whenever you need it.
