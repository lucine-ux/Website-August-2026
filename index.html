About Lucine

I know what it's liketo look fine on the outside.

Breathwork taught me how to soften, quiet the noise, and reconnect with the calm that was always there underneath.

How it works

Slow breathing that builds over time.

The breathwork I teach is gentle and slow, and designed to let your nervous system lead. Nothing is forced. Long held stress begins to unwind at your own pace, as much as your system is ready for.

One session can feel profound. An ongoing practice is where the change lives. Over time, your body starts to reach for the new pattern first. That doesn't happen through effort. It happens through repetition, breath by breath.

Gentle, slow breathing

A style that tells your nervous system it can soften. Nothing is pushed or forced, so your body can let go of what it's been holding.

Practice that builds

Repeated breathwork plants new patterns. Over time, your system stops defaulting to stress and starts moving toward the life you actually want.

Something you can sustain

An ongoing practice that builds your capacity, so there's more room for what matters to you.
