Questions

When does my $47 price end?

4 days after your invite email. After that ADHDecompress is $67 USD.

Do I have to start straight away?

No. Your 2 weeks start the day you join, so pick a time that works for you.

Are the sessions really live?

Yes. They're my real twice weekly sessions. Only the 3 starter lessons are recorded.

Why do I watch the lessons first?

So you know what to expect before you lie down and breathe with me. It makes the first live session feel a lot easier.

What if I can't make the live sessions?

Every session is recorded. You don't fall behind.

What if I've never done breathwork?

Most people haven't. It's gentle and I talk you through it.

What if big feelings come up?

I don't push for big feelings. You're in control and you can go as slowly as you like.

Is this instead of therapy?

No. It sits alongside therapy and medication.

Is breathwork safe for me?

It isn't right for everyone. If you're pregnant, or you have a heart condition, epilepsy, high blood pressure, glaucoma or a psychiatric diagnosis, email me before you join so we can talk it through. This is a wellbeing practice. It isn't medical treatment, therapy or crisis support.
