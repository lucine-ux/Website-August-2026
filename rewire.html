Is this for you?

Let's be honest
about the fit.

This is for you if

  • You've tried the mindset stuff and it hasn't shifted
  • You're exhausted in a way that sleep doesn't fix
  • You have ADHD or suspect you do and standard wellness advice doesn't land
  • You want to understand what's actually happening in your body
  • You've had moments of regulation before and you want more of that
  • You're ready to show up consistently, even on hard weeks

This isn't for you if

  • You're looking for a quick fix you can set and forget
  • You want someone to tell you what to think or feel
  • You're in acute mental health crisis (please reach out to a professional)
  • You're looking for a magic pill that does the work without you showing up

Got questions?

The ones people actually ask.

Do I need any experience with breathwork?

No. Every session is facilitated and I guide you through everything. You'll find your feet within the first session.

What if I can't make the live sessions?

Every session is recorded and available in your replay library within 24 hours. The live component is great but the practice works just as well on replay. You won't fall behind by missing a session.

What does a typical session look like?

Sessions run 60 minutes on Zoom. We do a short check in, around 40 minutes of active breathwork, and time to integrate and share if you want to. You don't have to have your camera on and you never have to share anything you don't want to.

When do sessions run?

There are two live sessions every week. I share the days and times when you join. Every session is recorded and in your library within 24 hours, so if the live time doesn't suit your week, the replay works just as well.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel anytime before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. No lock in contracts and no penalties.

Is this therapy?

No. This is breathwork and nervous system education. I'm not a therapist and The Rewire is not a substitute for mental health support. If you're in a mental health crisis please reach out to a professional. For most people the two work really well alongside each other.
