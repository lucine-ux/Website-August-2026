You hold it together all day. Then one small thing tips you over.

  • Your ADHD brain is full by 3pm
  • You're exhausted but you can't switch off
  • Someone asks one more question and you snap, then feel awful about it
  • You've tried apps, journals and meditation. They work for about four days
  • From the outside you look fine. Nobody can tell how close to the edge you are

You know what to do. You know you should stay calm. Your body just won't let you.

Planners and willpower work from the top down, through your thinking brain. Breathwork works from the bottom up, through your body. That's why it reaches the bit the planners can't.

How a Rewire session works.

1

Set your intention

You get really clear on how you'd prefer things to be. It helps your brain know what to look for.

2

Breathe

I guide you into a gentle breath through the nose. Then it's you, your breath and an epic playlist for about 40 minutes. Eyes closed, usually lying down. Your mind is allowed to wander, which for an ADHD brain is a delight.

3

Notice what comes up

Mostly it's body sensations. Buzzing, warmth, tingling, a tightness that moves or melts. Sometimes it's a picture or a random story that seems to make no sense. That's your nervous system showing you old patterns it's been holding onto. Quiet sessions count too.

4

The debrief with me

This is the real key. I help you see how what you felt links to the thing you want to change. That's the aha moment. When an old pattern comes up and you make sense of it, your brain gets the chance to file it in a new way. That's how it starts to rewire.

Most people turn their camera off. You never have to share anything you don't want to.

Why it's live, and why it's a group.

I've done breathwork for six years and I still book my own supported sessions.

Your body settles faster in a room where other bodies are settling. Someone helps you get your intention right, which is harder than it sounds. And you get to say what came up out loud and have it reflected back, because none of us can see our own stuff.

One session can shift how you feel that day. Changing the pattern takes repetition. Your body has been holding this for years and it hands it over layer by layer. Twice a week, with people who notice when you're not there, is what keeps you coming back on the hard weeks.

Questions

Do I need any breathwork experience?

No. Most people haven't done it before. It's gentle and I talk you through it.

Is it a bit woo woo?

Karyn thought so too. Her words: "Sounds a little bit woowoo, but if you're asking me does it work? Yes. It does." It's breathing, body sensations and a conversation afterwards. Nobody asks you to clear your mind.

When are the live sessions?

Mondays 6am and Wednesdays 5pm Sydney time. That's a different day and time where you are, so check the times shown in the "What you get" section above. Workshop times change, and I share them inside.

What if I can't make it live?

Every session is recorded, so you can breathe with the replay when it suits you. Live is where the debrief happens, so come when you can.

Can't I just do breathwork on YouTube?

You can, and it's one practice on its own. The debrief is what you can't get from a video. That's where I help you understand what came up for you, and it's the part that makes it stick.

What if I feel nothing?

That's okay. Some sessions are big and some are very quiet. Quiet sessions still count.

Do I have to have my camera on or share?

No. Most people turn their camera off. You can just listen in the debrief.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time before your next billing date and you won't be charged again. No lock in.

Is this therapy?

No. It's breathwork and nervous system education, and it sits alongside therapy and medication. If you're in crisis, please start with your doctor or a crisis line.

Is breathwork safe for me?

It isn't right for everyone. If you're pregnant, or you have a heart condition, epilepsy, high blood pressure, glaucoma or a psychiatric diagnosis, email me before you join so we can talk it through. This is a wellbeing practice. It isn't medical treatment, therapy or crisis support.

Still not sure?

Email me at lucine@backtobreath.net.au, or send me a DM on Instagram with the word REWIRE. Tell me where you're at and I'll give you an honest answer.
