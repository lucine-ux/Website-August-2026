The ADHD Capacity Program · Waitlist

Stop surviving your days. Start choosing them.

The ADHD Capacity Program is 6 months of guided mentorship for ADHD folks who are done just surviving their days. Live group breathwork, structured learning and a community that gets it.

Join the waitlist See what's inside

Small group. Cohorts twice a year. Waitlist members hear first.

Two weeks helps you come down. Six months changes what you can hold.

Lots of ADHD folks feel better after a few sessions, then slide back when life gets loud. A nervous system that has been on high alert for years needs more than a quick fix. It needs time, repetition and someone guiding you through it.

The ADHD Capacity Program is for when you're ready to build real capacity: more room for stress, for interruptions and for things going wrong, without losing your day to them.

What's inside

Six months, held in one place.

01

Live group breathwork

Two live sessions a week with me, online, for the full 6 months.

02

Structured learning

Short pre-recorded lessons to watch in your own time, so there's a thread running through the sessions.

03

Guided mentorship

Monthly check-ins in the group, where you can ask questions and get support.

04

Proof it's working

You retake the nervous system audit at the start, middle and end, so you can see what's changing.

05

A community that gets it

A small group of ADHD folks doing this alongside you.

06

Every session recorded

Miss one? Watch the replay. You never fall behind.

Who this is for

  • ADHD folks who are tired of running on empty
  • You've felt the shift from breathwork and want it to last
  • You want support, without another thing to keep up with
  • You'd rather be in a small group than go it alone

What to know

  • It's a live group program. It isn't 1:1 coaching
  • It sits alongside therapy and medication, it doesn't replace them
  • New to breathwork? Start with ADHDecompress first

Experience and education, not medical advice.
