Before you start

  • Lie down or sit back somewhere soft. Your bed is perfect.
  • Headphones are nice, but your phone speaker is fine too.
  • Press play, close your eyes and let my voice do the rest. You don't need to know anything first.
  • Your mind will wander off. That's ADHD, and it's fine. Just come back to my voice.
  • When it ends, take your time opening your eyes.

If you feel dizzy or tingly, stop and breathe normally for a bit. If you're pregnant or have a heart or lung condition, check with your doctor first.
